GDM privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

GDM ("the app") is a personal gestational-diabetes diary for Android, built for one user.

What the app stores

All of this is stored only on the phone:

  • glucose readings from a FreeStyle Libre sensor, received from the GlucoDataHandler app on the same phone;
  • meals and measurements you enter, share into the app, or import from files you choose;
  • meals you plan in the app, and meals read from your GDM Google Doc or Google Sheet (see below);
  • app settings.

Google user data

  • If you connect a Google account, the app requests one permission: drive.file, which covers only the Drive files that the app creates or that you open with it.
  • The app uses it to create a document and/or a spreadsheet in your Google Drive, each holding only a header line, and to read the meals that Gemini writes into them. When you start, change or delete a planned meal or one of its foods in the app, the app deletes its lines from them, so they hold only what you haven't eaten yet. The app writes nothing else to them, and it cannot see your other files.
  • Meals read from them are stored only on your phone, with the meals you plan in the app, and in backups you save. They are not shared, sold or transferred to anyone. They are not used for advertising, and nobody reads them.
  • GDM's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Disconnecting in the app's settings revokes this access. You can also remove it at myaccount.google.com/connections. The document and the spreadsheet stay in your Drive until you delete them.

What leaves the phone

  • The app connects only to GlucoDataHandler on the same phone (127.0.0.1) and, if you connected a Google account, to Google's Docs and Sheets APIs for your meal document and spreadsheet.
  • Backups and CSV exports are written only to a file or folder that you choose.
  • The app has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code, and Android cloud backup is off.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling deletes all app data from the phone. Backup files you saved stay where you put them.

Changes

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new date.
