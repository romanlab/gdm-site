GDM

Gestational Diabetes Monitor — a personal diary app for Android.

GDM shows glucose from a FreeStyle Libre sensor next to the meals she logs, and reports the 1-hour and 2-hour values after each meal and the morning fasting value. Meals can be entered in the app, shared into it, or read from a Google Sheet that Gemini fills in.

GDM is a private app for one user, distributed through Google Play internal testing. All data is stored on the phone.
